Executive Summary

1Executive summary

gogermany.ma has six paid integrations that bill per-use. Two of them — CV improvement and Anschreiben generation — drive ~80% of variable cost. Everything else is negligible at any realistic scale. One paid consultation booking covers ~50+ heavy users for a full day.

Key findings

  • Two endpoints dominate cost — /api/cv-ai and /api/generate-anschreiben use Claude Sonnet 4.5 ($3 / $15 per million input/output tokens). One CV-AI call ≈ 40× the cost of one writing-correction call.
  • The daily reading + writing exercises are essentially free — Claude Haiku 4.5 at ~$0.002 per call, capped at one per level per UTC day (5 maximum per user per day per exercise type).
  • Photo enhancement is bounded — Replicate's google/nano-banana at ~$0.04 per call, behind an entitlement gate (paid credits required after the daily quota).
  • One €16 consultation pays for ~80 to 800 heavy-user days of API spend. The economics work even at a 1% conversion rate.
Bottom line. The platform's variable cost is small, well-bounded by entitlements, and easily covered by consultation revenue. The biggest cost lever — if needed — is tightening the daily free quota on Sonnet endpoints.

5Recommendations

Cost monitoring
Track 5 KPIs weekly

Daily Anthropic spend (split Sonnet vs Haiku); Replicate spend; CV/Anschreiben call counts; free-quota exhaustion rate; weekly bookings.

Cost lever 1
Tighten Sonnet free quota

If costs grow uncomfortable, drop the daily free quota on CV/Anschreiben from 3 to 1, then introduce paid credits (€2 / extra call). Haiku endpoints stay generous.

Cost lever 2
Cache aggressively

Daily exercises already cache per-day per user in localStorage. Consider edge-caching CV-AI prompts where the same input recurs — minimal but helpful at scale.

Revenue lever
Surface consultations more

Every €16 booking = ~50–500× a heavy user-day. Even modest conversion rate improvements outpace any cost-cutting effort.

Watch threshold
Vercel Pro at 60 K invocations

Set an alert at 60 K monthly invocations (60% of free cap). Same for Supabase row-reads / egress. Gives you 2–3 weeks runway to upgrade smoothly.

Long-term
Move to Cal.com when ready

If you ever need multiple Calendly event types, switch to Cal.com (free, unlimited event types) instead of upgrading Calendly to Standard ($120/yr).
