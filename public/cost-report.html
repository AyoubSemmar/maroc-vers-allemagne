Executive Summary

1Executive summary

gogermany.ma has six paid integrations that bill per-use. Daily free quotas have been tightened (v1.1) so heavy users now cap at ~$0.25/day — a 39% reduction from v1.0. CV and Anschreiben endpoints (Sonnet) remain the dominant variable cost. One paid consultation booking covers ~70+ heavy users for a full day.

Key findings

  • Two endpoints dominate cost — /api/cv-ai and /api/generate-anschreiben use Claude Sonnet 4.5 ($3 / $15 per million input/output tokens). One CV-AI call ≈ 40× the cost of one writing-correction call. Both are now strictly capped at 1 free per day.
  • The daily reading + writing exercises remain cheap — Claude Haiku 4.5 at ~$0.002 per call, capped at 2 per level per UTC day (10 max per user per day per exercise type). Doubled the cap from v1.0 because Haiku is cheap enough that it doesn't move the needle.
  • Photo enhancement is bounded — Replicate's google/nano-banana at ~$0.04 per call, capped at 2 free per day (down from 3). Paid credits required afterwards.
  • One €16 consultation pays for ~70 to 1,000 heavy-user days of API spend. The economics work even at a 1% conversion rate.
Bottom line. v1.1 daily caps cut worst-case user cost by ~39% while keeping the user experience generous. Sonnet endpoints (the cost driver) are now strictly 1/day. Haiku endpoints (high-engagement) doubled to 2/day at near-zero marginal cost.

5Recommendations

Cost monitoring
Track 5 KPIs weekly

Daily Anthropic spend (split Sonnet vs Haiku); Replicate spend; CV/Anschreiben call counts; free-quota exhaustion rate; weekly bookings.

Cost lever 1 — applied ✅
Sonnet daily quota now 1/day

Previously 3/day for CV/Anschreiben. v1.1 dropped it to 1/day. Paid credits (€2 / extra call) take over after the daily allowance — generates revenue from heavy users.

Cost lever 2
Cache aggressively

Daily exercises already cache per-day per user in localStorage. Consider edge-caching CV-AI prompts where the same input recurs — minimal but helpful at scale.

Revenue lever
Surface consultations more

Every €16 booking = ~50–500× a heavy user-day. Even modest conversion rate improvements outpace any cost-cutting effort.

Watch threshold
Vercel Pro at 60 K invocations

Set an alert at 60 K monthly invocations (60% of free cap). Same for Supabase row-reads / egress. Gives you 2–3 weeks runway to upgrade smoothly.

Long-term
Move to Cal.com when ready

If you ever need multiple Calendly event types, switch to Cal.com (free, unlimited event types) instead of upgrading Calendly to Standard ($120/yr).
